# Indian Fundamentals

## Shared evidence

Before collecting or interpreting inputs, read
[evidence and freshness](../india-research-core/references/evidence.md) and
[data access](../india-research-core/references/data-access.md). All sibling skills
are distributed with this suite. Load other procedures only when the task needs
them; apply them in the current session without assuming additional agents exist.

Read the relevant [sector playbook](../india-research-core/references/sector-playbooks.md).

## Workflow

1. Map revenue/profit by segment, geography, customer, product and distribution.
   Explain unit economics, competitive advantage, dependencies, substitutes and
   what drives volume, price, margins, reinvestment and cash generation.
2. Build comparable annual and quarterly tables from filings, normally 5 years
   and 8 quarters if available. Reconcile restatements and consolidated basis.
   Track revenue, operating profit, attributable PAT, diluted EPS, CFO, capex,
   free cash flow, working capital, debt/cash and sector-specific KPIs.
3. Decompose growth into organic volume/pricing, FX, acquisitions and accounting
   changes. Explain normalized margins, ROE/ROCE/ROIC definitions and whether
   returns exceed reinvestment costs. Banks/insurers need their own metrics.
4. Assess management incentives and delivery, capital allocation, dilution,
   dividends/buybacks, investments, liquidity and refinancing needs. Separate
   management claims from demonstrated economics.
5. Explain quality, durability, main growth constraints, balance-sheet resilience,
   3–5 key thesis variables and falsification evidence. Link forensic concerns to
   `india-forensic-analysis` and pricing questions to `india-stock-valuation`.

Deliver a sourced business/financial assessment and open questions. Business
quality alone does not establish attractiveness at the current share price.
