# Indian Market News Roundup

## Shared evidence

Before collecting or interpreting inputs, read
[evidence and freshness](../india-research-core/references/evidence.md) and
[data access](../india-research-core/references/data-access.md). All sibling skills
are distributed with this suite. Load other procedures only when the task needs
them; apply them in the current session without assuming additional agents exist.

Read the [news report contract](../india-research-core/references/report-contracts.md).

1. Set explicit interval and session cutoff. Search official company/exchange,
   regulator, RBI, government and economic releases; supplement with accessible
   original reporting. Include global events relevant to Indian exposures.
2. Cover corporate results/guidance, orders/M&A, governance/audit/credit events,
   regulation/policy, rates, inflation/growth, currency/commodities and sector
   developments. Company-focused requests include smaller relevant issuers, not
   only index heavyweights. Do not promise every news item was collected.
3. Record event date, publication/update time, source and evidence; deduplicate
   syndicated versions and distinguish a new development from recycled history.
4. Rank by likely earnings/cash-flow/risk/discount-rate impact, affected exposure,
   surprise and horizon. Explain the ranking; sentiment words are not analysis.
5. For each material item, give what happened, why it matters, beneficiaries and
   vulnerabilities, observed price/volume reaction if established, and next
   evidence. Do not attribute a whole market move to a coincident headline.
6. Close with upcoming results/policy/data calendar and coverage gaps. Use
   `india-news-impact-analysis` for substantial scenarios and indirect effects.

Deliver a concise ranked digest with links, not copied articles. No background
delivery or external messages are started by a roundup request.
