# Indian Peer Comparison

## Shared evidence

Before collecting or interpreting inputs, read
[evidence and freshness](../india-research-core/references/evidence.md) and
[data access](../india-research-core/references/data-access.md). All sibling skills
are distributed with this suite. Load other procedures only when the task needs
them; apply them in the current session without assuming additional agents exist.

Read [sector playbooks](../india-research-core/references/sector-playbooks.md).

1. Define the question and choose peers by business mix, geography, scale,
   customers and economics. Explain inclusions/exclusions; group dissimilar
   subsegments rather than rank them as if identical.
2. Standardize date, fiscal periods, consolidated basis, currency, lease/dilution
   treatment and metric definitions. Preserve unavailable/non-comparable entries.
3. Compare growth quality, normalized profitability, returns, cash conversion,
   financial risk, governance, sector KPIs and valuation on the same price date.
4. Explain premium/discount drivers and whether durable differences justify them.
   Rank for specific criteria and horizons, not a universal low-P/E winner.
5. For an investment-choice request, assess each at the cited price with scenarios,
   confidence and timeframe; distinguish the best business from best priced choice.

Deliver a comparison matrix, key tradeoffs, data caveats and research priorities.
Use `india-stock-research` for a full standalone recommendation on a candidate.
