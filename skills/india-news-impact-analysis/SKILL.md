# Indian News Impact Analysis

## Shared evidence

Before collecting or interpreting inputs, read
[evidence and freshness](../india-research-core/references/evidence.md) and
[data access](../india-research-core/references/data-access.md). All sibling skills
are distributed with this suite. Load other procedures only when the task needs
them; apply them in the current session without assuming additional agents exist.

1. Verify the event and what changed, original source, dates, policy/document
   scope and implementation conditions. Separate rumors, proposals and enacted
   changes. Do not accept a user-supplied headline as verified fact.
2. Map direct effects on demand, realizations, costs, working capital, financing,
   regulation and discount rates. Trace indirect effects through competitors,
   suppliers, customers, currency and policy responses.
3. Quantify company exposures from disclosed sensitivities/segments when possible.
   Give ranges and assumptions; avoid multiplying unsupported guessed exposures.
4. Build base/upside/downside paths with timeframes and observable milestones.
   Compare current market expectations and observed reaction only where supported.
   An economically beneficial event may already be priced into the stock.
5. Identify affected companies for further research, valuation/thesis changes,
   counterarguments and evidence that would overturn each scenario. Scenario
   probabilities require justification; otherwise leave paths unweighted.

Deliver an event-to-financial-impact map and conditional investment implications.
For “is it a good bet now?” apply `india-stock-research` rather than extrapolate
from one favorable headline.
