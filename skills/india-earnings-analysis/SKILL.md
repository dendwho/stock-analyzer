# Indian Earnings Review

## Shared evidence

Before collecting or interpreting inputs, read
[evidence and freshness](../india-research-core/references/evidence.md) and
[data access](../india-research-core/references/data-access.md). All sibling skills
are distributed with this suite. Load other procedures only when the task needs
them; apply them in the current session without assuming additional agents exist.

Use the filed result, notes, presentation, and call transcript/audio when
accessible. Apply [sector KPIs](../india-research-core/references/sector-playbooks.md).

## Workflow

1. Confirm period, standalone/consolidated basis and restatements. Compare YoY and
   QoQ with seasonal context; derive Q4 from annual minus nine months only with
   matched basis and label it. TTM must contain four nonoverlapping quarters.
2. Bridge revenue, operating margin and PAT to volume, price, mix, FX, acquisition,
   cost and exceptional items. Separate tax, other income and accounting effects.
3. Reconcile cash conversion, working capital, debt and sector operating KPIs.
   Compare with explicit prior guidance and authentic accessible consensus only.
4. Extract guidance changes, questions avoided, capex/hiring/demand signals and
   risks from the call, distinguishing management opinion from verified facts.
5. Update the existing thesis and forecast assumptions; explain which changed,
   whether the investment horizon moved, and why entry/valuation should be revisited.
   Compare price reaction to the correct release time, not an unrelated session.

Deliver a results bridge, management-commitment tracker, material surprises,
unresolved questions and implications. If no transcript exists, state the gap;
do not fabricate call quotations or analyst expectations.
