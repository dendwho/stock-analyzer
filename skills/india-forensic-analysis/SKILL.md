# Indian Forensic Research

## Shared evidence

Before collecting or interpreting inputs, read
[evidence and freshness](../india-research-core/references/evidence.md) and
[data access](../india-research-core/references/data-access.md). All sibling skills
are distributed with this suite. Load other procedures only when the task needs
them; apply them in the current session without assuming additional agents exist.

Use annual-report notes, cash-flow statements, exchange disclosures, auditor
reports and original credit-rating rationales. This is evidence review, not an
assertion of fraud.

## Workflow

1. Reconcile PAT to operating cash over multiple years. Investigate receivables,
   inventory, unbilled revenue, advances, capitalized costs, other income, one-offs,
   changing depreciation/policies and unexplained segment changes.
2. Trace debt, cash restrictions, guarantees, contingent liabilities, subsidiary
   funding, acquisitions, impairment, dilution and potential off-balance obligations.
3. Read audit opinion, qualifications, key audit matters, internal-control findings,
   auditor changes and explanations. Track inconsistencies across report versions.
4. Compare promoter holdings/pledges and institutional changes at dated periods.
   Inspect related-party flows, remuneration, warrants/preferential issues, exits,
   material litigations and exchange/regulatory findings from primary evidence.
5. For each flag, record exact evidence/page, magnitude, trend, benign alternative,
   management explanation, potential valuation impact and what could resolve it.
   Distinguish confirmed findings, unexplained anomalies and unsupported allegations.

Deliver severity-ranked evidence and open questions. Aggregate ratios can flag
investigation needs but cannot prove manipulation. Avoid an automatic numerical
fraud score. Withhold conclusions for inaccessible notes rather than declaring
the company clean.
