# Indian Sector Research

## Shared evidence

Before collecting or interpreting inputs, read
[evidence and freshness](../india-research-core/references/evidence.md) and
[data access](../india-research-core/references/data-access.md). All sibling skills
are distributed with this suite. Load other procedures only when the task needs
them; apply them in the current session without assuming additional agents exist.

Read the relevant [sector playbook](../india-research-core/references/sector-playbooks.md).

1. Define sector/subsector scope and value chain, economic profit pools, demand
   drivers, substitutes, market shares and barriers. Attribute market-size and
   growth estimates and their methodology; do not treat sponsored forecasts as fact.
2. Investigate domestic/global demand, supply/capacity, inventories, pricing,
   financing, policy/regulation, imports/exports and relevant commodities/currency.
3. Establish cycle position using multiple leading and lagging indicators. Compare
   structural opportunity against near-term supply or valuation risks.
4. Compare representative companies and subsegments on operating KPIs, returns,
   leverage, execution and governance; use `india-peer-comparison` if needed.
5. Compare sector returns/relative strength, participation and historical valuation
   with methodology changes noted. A rising index is not proof of broad strength.
6. Develop base/upside/downside sector scenarios, beneficiaries/vulnerabilities,
   lead indicators, horizon and monitoring triggers. Separate business outlook
   from current investment pricing and identify where evidence is weak.

Deliver an industry map, competitive/valuation matrix, cycle assessment and
conditional company research shortlist.
