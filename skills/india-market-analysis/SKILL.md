# Indian Market Conditions

## Shared evidence

Before collecting or interpreting inputs, read
[evidence and freshness](../india-research-core/references/evidence.md) and
[data access](../india-research-core/references/data-access.md). All sibling skills
are distributed with this suite. Load other procedures only when the task needs
them; apply them in the current session without assuming additional agents exist.

1. Identify session/cutoff and latest completed exchange trading day. Collect
   relevant broad, size and sector indices, historical returns, India VIX,
   valuation series and dated flow observations where available.
2. Assess trend and relative performance across large/mid/small caps. Compare
   index returns with participation: advances/declines, new highs/lows and shares
   above relevant averages from a defined, dated universe. Do not substitute US
   breadth or infer all-stock breadth from Nifty 50 alone.
3. Evaluate sector leadership/rotation, dispersion, liquidity, volatility and
   valuation percentiles on a consistent methodology. Mention index accounting
   or constituent changes that compromise historical comparisons.
4. Identify FPI/FII/DII flow source and coverage: exchange provisional cash data
   and depository investment data are different series; don't add them as unique
   flows or claim institutional intent from totals.
5. State supportive/mixed/adverse conditions with observed evidence and contrary
   indicators. Explain implications for pullback/breakout entries and exposure
   planning, plus catalysts and review triggers. Avoid arbitrary regime scores
   or fixed allocation prescriptions unsupported by portfolio context.

Deliver session summary, evidence table, sector map and conditional outlook.
Use `india-market-news-roundup` for headlines and `india-trade-planning` for an
individual entry. Missing breadth means unknown breadth, not healthy breadth.
